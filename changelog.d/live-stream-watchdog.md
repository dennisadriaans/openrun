- Leaving the app idle no longer leaves it stuck. A live stream that dies quietly — laptop asleep, Wi-Fi switched, dev server restarted — used to stay `OPEN` as far as the browser was concerned, so no error ever fired, polling stayed switched off, and pages you moved to showed stale data forever. The browser now judges liveness by the server's heartbeat rather than by the socket: a stream that goes quiet is torn down and redialled, reconnects immediately when the tab becomes visible or the network returns, and refetches what it missed while it was down.
- The cloud relay no longer wedges after a sleep. A dial that neither connected nor failed used to leave it "connecting" forever, and nothing retried; every attempt now has a deadline.
- Dev builds carry a live-connection indicator in the bottom-left corner. Toggle it to see every open stream, how long since its last frame, how many times it has reconnected, and the cloud relay's state — plus a button to redial the lot. It is compiled out of production builds.
