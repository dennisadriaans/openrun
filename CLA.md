## Why this exists (plain English, not part of the agreement)

Open Run is dual-licensed. The code in this repository is
[AGPLv3](./LICENSE), and we separately offer it under commercial terms to
organisations whose policies forbid AGPL (see
[COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](./COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md)). Offering those commercial
terms is only legally possible if one party can license the entire codebase.

If copyright stayed distributed across every contributor — which is what a DCO
sign-off does — then every future commercial licence would require tracking down
and getting agreement from every person who ever landed a patch. In practice that
means it never happens, and the project loses its only funding mechanism that
isn't donations.

So: **you keep ownership of your contribution.** You grant us a broad licence to
use it, including under other licence terms. You can still use, relicense,
publish or sell your own contribution however you like — this is a licence, not
an assignment.

What we commit to in return: the code in this repository stays open source under
AGPLv3. See the
[README](./README.md#open-source) for the standing boundary.

## How to sign

Open a pull request. A bot will comment asking you to reply with a single line:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your GitHub username, the commit, and a timestamp are recorded in a separate
public signatures repository. You sign once; it covers all your future
contributions.

**Contributing on behalf of a company?** If your employer owns the IP in your
work, you need a Corporate CLA instead. Open a Discussion and we will sort it
out.
